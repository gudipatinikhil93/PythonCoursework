Arithmetic operators
------------------------
1. Addition ex: a+b
2. Subtraction ex: a-b
3. Multiplication ex: a*b
4. Division ex: a/b note: output will be in float
5. Floor Division ex: a//b note: decimal part is removed
6. Modulo ex: a%b note: it is the reminder part in division

Comparison operators
--------------------------
1. Greater than ex: a>b
2. Less than ex: a<b
3. Greater than or equal to ex: a>=b
4. Less than or equal to ex: a<=b
5. Equal to Equal to ex: a==b
6. Not Equal to ex: a!=b 
all the outputs will be in Boolean only

Bitwise operators
--------------------------
1. & ex: a&b note: all the bits of the numbers should result in True
2. | ex: a|b note: any one of the bits should result in True
3. ~ ex: the positive results in negative and vice versa
4. ^ ex: a^b note: different bits give 1, same bits give 0
5. >> ex: a>>b note: bits are shifted to right
6. << ex: a<<b note: bits are shifted to left

Membership operators
---------------------------
1. in ex: 's' in 'aeiou'
2. not in ex: a not in b

Identity Operators
--------------------------
1. id(a) note: it shows the object reference
